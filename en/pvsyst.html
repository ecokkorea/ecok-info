PVsyst simulation at the design stage

What goes in, what comes out, and how it connects to string sizing and ESS.

Short answer

PVsyst calculates hourly generation, loss items and performance ratio from module and inverter specifications, string configuration, tilt and azimuth, local weather data and surrounding shade. ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do reviews the PVsyst result together with the string sizing report, and lays the hourly generation over the factory 15 minute consumption to size ESS and check economics. In a review of about 550 kWp on a factory roof in Chungnam, the PVsyst annual yield of about 720 MWh was fed into HOMER Grid to compare PV only with PV and ESS under the same conditions. Simulation values are planning values, so we compare them with actual generation after completion. Send us a layout or roof photos and the module and inverter models, and we start with the yield review. Phone +82 1555-2859.

What goes into PVsyst

InputContent
Module datasheetPower, voltage, temperature coefficients
Inverter specificationInput voltage range, number of MPPTs
String configurationModules per string, strings in parallel
InstallationTilt, azimuth, row spacing
Weather dataIrradiation and temperature at the site
ShadeNearby buildings, front rows, rooftop equipment
ResultsAnnual and monthly yield, loss items, performance ratio

Checked together with string sizing

  • We check the string configuration as well as the yield.
  • Example: 20 modules with 51.38 V open circuit voltage in series give 1,027.6 V in winter, above the 1,000 V inverter maximum input. In that case we reduce it to 19 or fewer.
  • Send model names and capacity and we review the string sizing report and PVsyst yield together.

Case: factory roof in Chungnam

  • About 550 kWp on three factory buildings in Chungnam (client not named, figures rounded).
  • PVsyst annual yield about 720 MWh (about 3.6 hours a day on average), all used on site.
  • HOMER Grid then compared PV only with PV and ESS (about 630 kWh, PCS about 210 kW) under the same conditions.
  • Hourly yield laid over the factory 15 minute consumption shows whether a morning peak remains and whether surplus power appears at midday. This is the basis for ESS sizing.

Simulation values are planning values

  • PVsyst results are expected values under design conditions.
  • Actual yield varies with weather, soiling, faults and curtailment.
  • After completion we compare with actual generation and look for the cause if the gap is large.
