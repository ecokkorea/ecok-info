Insights

Engineering notes from our Korean projects: tariffs, permitting, net metering, the agrivoltaics act, storage, energy yield, structures and quantities. Our own calculations and tools, no client names.

    Since September 2026 Korean local governments can only apply house setbacks of up to 200 m and a few zone setbacks to solar. How we measure, count and report them.

    Korean industrial tariffs bill the highest 15 minute demand for a year. Our method: a full year of KEPCO data, two dispatch models and a capacity sweep.

    Same weather, same module, same 3D scene: the two programs agreed within 2.2% unshaded and 0.24 points on shading loss. What the gap means for finance.

    Column grid, 4 m clearance, Korean wind and snow codes and a 3D check that changed the column size. A worked agrivoltaic design of about 1 MW.
