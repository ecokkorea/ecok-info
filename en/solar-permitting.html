Solar permitting in Korea

Generation license, construction plan notification and pre-use inspection, with dates from two factory rooftops.

Short answer

A solar plant in Korea passes three gates after the site is chosen. First the electricity generation business license, which records capacity, installation area and the preparation period; if capacity or area changes, an amendment comes first (Electric Utility Act, Article 9). Second the construction plan notification, with a KESCO pre-technical review for every PV plant except those under 500 kW connected at low voltage (Enforcement Rule of the Electric Utility Act, Appended Table 8, Note 3). Third the pre-use inspection by KESCO, applied for at least 7 days before the inspection date (Enforcement Rule of the Electric Utility Act, Article 31(5)). On a 558 kW factory roof in Cheonan handled by ECO K KOREA, these took about one year from the license in April 2025 to commercial operation on March 20, 2026. Phone +82 1555-2859.

1. Generation business license

  • Factory A in Cheonan received its license from the city on April 1, 2025: capacity and installation area, conditional license, preparation period until September 30, 2026.
  • Because capacity and area are both written on the license, a change to either needs an amendment first. Plant and business must start within the preparation period (Electric Utility Act, Article 9(1)).
  • After a closer roof review one building could not take modules, so an amendment in August 2025 cut capacity from 737.1 kW to 560.16 kW.
  • A generator reports business commencement within 30 days of its first power trade (Electric Utility Act, Article 9(4)). Cheonan reported on April 7, 2026.

2. Construction plan notification

  • PV plants under 500 kW connected at low voltage may skip the pre-technical review report. Others (500 kW or more, or high voltage connection) get the KESCO pre-technical review first (Enforcement Rule of the Electric Utility Act, Appended Table 8, Note 3).
  • At Cheonan (558 kW), 5 of 15 review items needed additions: working space layout, flood protection and fencing for outdoor equipment, switchgear sections, grounding terminal box details, and module output polarity marking. Reviewed September 5, fixed September 8, found adequate.
  • String DC cables were checked to IEC 62548 (short circuit current × 1.25 = 22.48 A) with a separate ampacity review by number of circuits in the tray.
  • Construction plan and building construction start notifications were filed on the same day, September 17, 2025. The start notification was accepted on September 25 after a correction request on September 22.
  • When work runs long, file an amendment before the certificate period ends. Cheonan extended to March 31, 2026 on November 25, 2025. Changwon (1,349.12 kWp) moved from June 30 to July 31 because of the rainy season, a ready mix concrete strike and a cable route change.

3. Pre-use inspection

SitePre-use inspection and commercial operation
Factory A roof in Cheonan, Chungnam, 558 kW, 22.9 kVPassed February 11, 2026; commercial operation March 20, 2026
Factory A roof in Changwon, Gyeongnam, 1,349.12 kWp, 22.9 kVPassed July 23, 2026; commercial operation July 31, 2026
  • Apply to KESCO at least 7 days before the inspection date, attaching the construction plan acceptance, design documents and supervisor assignment confirmation, and the electrical safety manager appointment certificate (Enforcement Rule of the Electric Utility Act, Article 31(5)).
  • Both sites passed on the first attempt. At Cheonan, insulation resistance and open circuit voltage were checked on January 27, about two weeks before the inspection.
  • Documents handed in on the day at Cheonan: single line diagram, module and inverter certificates and test reports, serial number lists, supervisor assignment, process and excavation photos, safety manager appointment, cable test reports.
  • Inspection fees are not listed here; check with the local KESCO branch.

Statutes checked: Electric Utility Act as in force on 2026-09-18, Enforcement Rule as in force on 2026-10-16.
