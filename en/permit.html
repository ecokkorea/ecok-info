Site feasibility and permitting

Give us the lot number and we first check whether solar can be built there. We also prepare the permit documents and renderings.

Scope

Site feasibility

  • Slope, elevation and terrain section analysis
  • Local ordinance setbacks and special conditions
  • Farmland, forest and heritage regulations
  • Nearby grid facilities and hosting capacity

Development permit review

  • Feasibility and conditions
  • Construction and permitting cost review
  • Grading plan and module layout
  • Review report

Permit documents

  • Electricity generation business license and amendments
  • Development permit drawings
  • Construction plan notification

Renderings and 3D

  • Concept bird's eye views for residents and permits
  • SketchUp 3D models
  • V-Ray photorealistic renderings
