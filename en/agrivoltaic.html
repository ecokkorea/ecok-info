Agrivoltaics in Korea under the new act

Eligibility, design criteria and yield, read from the law effective December 17, 2026.

Short answer

Agrivoltaics was set in law for the first time when the Act on the Promotion and Support of Agrivoltaic Power Generation Business (영농형 태양광 발전사업의 활성화 및 지원에 관한 법률) (Act No. 21804) took effect on December 17, 2026. Eligible operators are farmers who live in the town (eup, myeon or dong) or an adjoining one and can prove three years of farm management, cooperatives of 10 or more residents, and agricultural corporations in renewable energy zones (Article 5). Sites are farmland outside agricultural promotion areas or farmland in renewable energy zones (Article 6). The period is set by the mayor or county head within 30 years from the permit date (Article 7). Draft enforcement decree and rules (pre-announced on September 16, 2026, not final) include a shading ratio under 30% and a minimum structure height of 2.5 m. Why construction costs more

  • Lifting modules to about 4 m makes columns and beams longer.
  • Lifting equipment, safety measures and foundation pull out tests are added.
  • Cost varies widely with height, tracking and the scope of electrical work. Actual cost needs a quote for each site.

Questions

QuestionAnswer
Can I get an agrivoltaic permit now?The act takes effect on December 17, 2026. Before that there is no permit under this act, so ask the city or county about the procedure first.
Can it go on rice fields in agricultural promotion zones?In principle, farmland outside agricultural promotion areas and in renewable energy zones. Cooperatives of 10 or more residents may use other farmland if the city or county consults the agriculture ministry (Art. 6(2)).
Can I lease the land?Yes. The landlord's consent is needed (Art. 8(1)), and while the permit is valid the landlord cannot refuse lease renewal without just cause (Art. 18).
What if I stop farming?You must farm every year as in the farming plan (Art. 13). Otherwise correction orders, suspension or cancellation, and penalty surcharges (up to KRW 500 million or three times the gain) can follow (Arts. 14 to 16).
Is the yield lower than normal solar?Less capacity fits on the same land because of the shading limit. But bifacial modules lifted high receive reflected light on the rear, so yield per kW did not drop (1,368 kWh/kWp in the design).
