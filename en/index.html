What ECO K KOREA does

ECO K KOREA (주식회사 에코케이코리아) is an electrical and energy engineering company headquartered in Naju, Jeollanam-do, Republic of Korea.

  • Site feasibility and permittingSlope, setbacks and land use checks, development permit review, license documents.
  • EPC electrical designSubstation, feeder, power and lighting design with calculation reports.
  • Construction supervisionDesign document review, site inspection and completion documents.
  • BOQ and quantity take-offDesign, progress, change order and as-built bills of quantities.
  • Fire alarm and telecomDrawings, take-off and BOQ for fire alarm, CCTV and structured cabling.
  • Protection relay coordinationFault current, relay settings and time current coordination curves.
  • Design calculatorsFree tools for lighting, fire detectors, PV string sizing and DC fuses.
  • ProjectsRecent projects and facilities our engineers have designed.
