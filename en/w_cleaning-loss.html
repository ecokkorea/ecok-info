Frequently asked questions

How much output do dirty solar panels lose?

The IEA PVPS Task 13 report (IEA-PVPS T13-21:2022, published January 2023) estimates that soiling cuts yearly solar output worldwide by 3 to 5%, with economic losses on the order of 3 to 5 billion euros a year. Dust, bird droppings, pollen and yellow dust, and how often it rains, vary a lot from site to site, so the most accurate value for your plant comes from comparing output before and after cleaning.

How often should panels be cleaned?

Only when the cleaning cost is less than the money lost to soiling. If the real loss is above the break even soiling loss % from this calculator, cleaning is worth it; if below, waiting for rain is better. More cleanings (⑥) mean more cost, so timing them for the dirtiest season (for example heavy pollen or yellow dust with little rain) is most efficient.

What rate should I enter?

If you sell the power, enter the SMP plus REC amount per kWh from your settlement statement. For self consumption, enter the energy rate per kWh of the electricity you avoid buying. Under net metering, enter the rate by which netting lowers your bill.

How do I find the specific yield per kW?

Divide last year's output from your monitoring system by the installed capacity (kW). Without data, refer to our 2026 PVsyst results at 8 Korean sites (roofs 1,169 to 1,322, ground mounted 1,255 to 1,367 kWh/kWp).

Can I clean the panels myself?

While the sun is up the DC side stays live even with the inverter off. Do not touch broken modules or exposed wiring, and avoid pressure washers and stiff brushes, which can damage the coating and frame seals. Rooftop work needs fall protection and trained people.

Notes (sources checked 2026-11-11)
  • Soiling loss range: IEA PVPS Task 13, "Soiling Losses: Impact on the Performance of Photovoltaic Power Plants" (IEA-PVPS T13-21:2022, published January 2023), key summary "estimated to cause a loss of annual PV energy production of 3-5%", economic loss "on the order of 3-5 billion euros".
  • Specific yield: 8 PVsyst 8.1.7 simulations by ECO K KOREA (KMA station typical year or PVGIS typical year), tabulated in our Korean insight #53.
  • No measured regional soiling statistics for Korea were found (unconfirmed), so ② is a user input and only the world average range is given for reference.
