Solar Pro Lesson 1: The four windows of the first screen and how it differs from PVsyst

The 3D CAD, I-V curve, energy and economics windows of the Solar Pro 4.8 first screen, and why its module by module shading calculation differs from PVsyst.

Lesson summary

The narration is in Korean with English subtitles (CC). The text below follows the narration. All examples are virtual plants.

Lesson 1 tours the four windows of the first screen and explains what sets Solar Pro apart from PVsyst. Press Open in the File menu and open House1, the house example included by the developer. All examples in this course are virtual plants with no site names or lot numbers.

The first window is 3D CAD, the scene where you place the house, modules and trees. Zoom with the mouse wheel and drag to rotate. The location, date and time at the top left read Kyoto, Japan, November 21, 4:37 p.m. The second window is the I-V curve, red for current and blue for power, showing what the modules produce at this very moment. The example has one inverter with 8 modules in series and 15 strings in parallel, 8.4 kW.

The third window is the energy graph, with monthly PV output and AC output as bars that can be switched between year, month and day. The fourth is the economics window with AC output and electricity bill savings. This course focuses on energy yield and shading.

Back in 3D CAD, Date and Time in the Shading menu takes the month, day, hour and minute. Set to the winter solstice, December 22 at 3 p.m., the shadows stretch out. The scene shown there is the example plant used in lesson 5, where seeing the shadows is covered in detail.

The difference between the two programs is how shade is calculated. PVsyst reduces shading per series string, so it is conservative and is the standard used by banks. Solar Pro calculates the I-V curve of every single module, so it is strong for shade that falls on only some modules, such as trees or nearby buildings. The same site gives different shading losses in the two programs, so we use PVsyst for financial evaluation and Solar Pro for sites with complex shading, and judge with both results side by side.

Frequently asked questions

How does Solar Pro differ from PVsyst?

PVsyst reduces shading per series string, which is conservative and the standard used by banks. Solar Pro calculates the I-V curve of every module, so it handles shade on only part of the array well.

What are the four windows on the Solar Pro first screen?

The 3D CAD scene, the I-V curve, the energy graph and the economics window. Changing the date and time in 3D CAD redraws the shadows.

Which program's result should I use?

The same site gives different shading losses. We use PVsyst for financial evaluation and Solar Pro for sites with complex shading, and judge with both results side by side.
