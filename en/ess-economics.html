PV and ESS economics with SAM, HOMER and REopt

One site, three independent tools, checked against each other.

Short answer

Yes. ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do calculates the same site separately with three tools, SAM and REopt from the US NREL and HOMER Grid, and checks them against each other. Inputs are the actual electricity bills and KEPCO 15 minute consumption data, and PV generation comes from PVsyst. You receive equipment sizes (kW, kWh), annual bill savings, payback and a 20 year cash flow for three cases: PV only, ESS only, and PV with ESS. Extracts of the original result reports from all three programs are attached at the back of the report so the calculation can be checked. Battery degradation, replacement timing and tariff changes are included. We can continue into design and construction. Three tools, cross-checked

  • SAM (US NREL): PV and ESS self consumption and bill savings
  • REopt (US NREL): optimal sizing and economics
  • HOMER Grid: dispatch matched to the tariff and load

Inputs

  • Actual electricity bills
  • KEPCO 15 minute consumption data (one year)
  • PV generation from PVsyst

What you receive

  • Equipment sizes (kW, kWh) for PV only, ESS only, and PV with ESS
  • Annual bill savings, payback and 20 year cash flow
  • Battery degradation, replacement timing and tariff changes
  • Extracts of the original reports from all three programs, attached
