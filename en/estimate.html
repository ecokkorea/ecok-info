Progress and change order BOQ preparation

For contractors and design offices: electrical, fire alarm and telecom.

Short answer

ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do (Electrical Construction Business Jeonnam-02344) prepares progress BOQs, change order BOQs and as-built BOQs for electrical work on behalf of contractors who won the tender and design offices, and covers fire alarm and telecom work as well. Send us the drawings, the contract (original design) BOQ in Excel and the schedule. We redo the quantity take-off, apply the contract unit prices and the standard estimating units (표준품셈) and standard market unit prices (표준시장단가), and deliver an Excel BOQ with quantity take-off sheets. For change orders we arrange a before and after quantity comparison with the reasons for each change, ready for discussion with the client. We also follow the client's required format. Our principal and engineers have done electrical design and BOQs for water treatment, roads, railways and substations for about 30 years. Phone +82 1555-2859, info@ecokkorea.co.kr
