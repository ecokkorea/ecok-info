Structural review for factory rooftop solar

What is checked, when reinforcement is needed, and the permits that follow.

Short answer

A structural review for factory rooftop solar checks dead load, live load, snow load, wind load and seismic load, and whether the anchors that join the existing building and the new structure can hold. The result decides which buildings and how much capacity can go up, and which permit documents are needed. On the 1,349.12 kWp factory roof in Changwon handled by ECO K KOREA, a licensed structural engineer confirmed a pull out force of 7.5 kN per anchor, and the existing corrugated steel roof was reinforced with an overlay steel sheet. On one building of the 558 kW factory roof in Cheonan, beams and columns were added, the structure was recalculated, and a major repair permit (대수선허가) and a structure construction notice (공작물축조신고) were granted together. At the city's request, the structural engineer rewrote the structural review confirmation with seismic items. Loads checked in the review

At factory A in Changwon, Gyeongnam (1,349.12 kWp), a licensed structural engineer checked five loads.

  • Dead load: weight of modules and structure
  • Live load: people on the roof for inspection and cleaning
  • Snow load
  • Wind load: uplift and pressure on modules
  • Seismic load

Each anchor between the existing building and the new structure was checked for a pull out force of 7.5 kN. The roof was corrugated metal sheet; an overlay steel sheet was added before a fixed aluminium alloy (6063-T5) structure was installed.

Reinforcement leads to a major repair permit

  • At factory A in Cheonan, Chungnam (558 kW), one building (floor area 485 m², single storey steel frame) could not carry the load, so beams and columns were added.
  • First structural calculation in May, recalculated when the buildings changed, second calculation with reinforcement on July 23, application for a major repair permit on July 29.
  • The city building department asked for a structural review confirmation in a form with seismic items, so the structural engineer rewrote and sealed it. The major repair permit and structure construction notice were processed together on September 1.
  • Seismic design confirmation: Korean building structural design code KDS 41, importance class 2, zone factor 0.22, base shear 34.2 kN and storey drift checked and found adequate (per the site documents).

Old slate roofs

  • The same Cheonan site also had a building with a slate roof completed in 1989.
  • Slate containing asbestos cracks easily when drilled, and standing on it is dangerous.
  • Three options were compared: steel overlay, removal and new roof, and installation on insulation board with a waterproof sheet.

Documents that speed things up

  • Building register and year of completion
  • Existing structural drawings (or a site survey if none)
  • Roof material and whether it contains asbestos
  • Drone or roof photos
  • Module count per building (used for the weight and area table)
  • Special notes in the structural calculation remain conditions for the whole operating life of the plant.
