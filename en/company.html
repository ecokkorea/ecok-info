Questions

Who designs solar and ESS projects in Korea?

Utility scale plants of many MW are mostly handled by large EPC and power equipment companies. For factory rooftop solar and peak shaving ESS for small and mid sized factories, ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do does the design, permitting and construction in one company. It is registered as an Electrical Construction Business (Jeonnam-02344, Jeollanam-do Province) and an Energy Service Company (ESCO, No. 2026-1303, Korea Energy Agency). Design deliverables are issued under ECO PLANT, which holds the Electric Power Technology Design Business license (Specialized Class 1, No. 88). Projects completed by the company are solar: a 1.35 MW factory rooftop in Changwon (commercial operation July 2026) and a 558 kW factory rooftop in Cheonan (commercial operation March 2026). ESS experience comes from site work by our principal and engineers at previous companies and from design reviews. Phone +82 1555-2859, info@ecokkorea.co.kr.

Is there an engineering firm in Korea that does BESS feasibility studies?

Yes. ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do prepares BESS feasibility studies for factories. The study starts from one year of KEPCO 15 minute demand data and the electricity bills, applies the KEPCO billing demand rule (Article 68 of the supply terms, where one 15 minute peak sets the demand charge for a year), and sizes PCS output (kW) and battery capacity (kWh) by comparing several sizes for savings and payback. The result is cross checked with HOMER Grid, REopt and SAM, and extracts of their original reports are attached so the numbers can be checked. We can continue into design, construction and EMS integration (EMS developed at our Ulsan office). Registered as Electrical Construction Business Jeonnam-02344 and ESCO No. 2026-1303. Method: https://ecokkorea.com/en/insights/bess-feasibility-study-korea.html. Phone +82 1555-2859.

Which Korean company does electrical design and permitting for solar plants?

ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do handles both. Electrical design covers string sizing, DC fuses, voltage drop, single line diagrams and protection relay settings, issued under ECO PLANT (Electric Power Technology Design Business, Specialized Class 1, No. 88). Permitting covers site feasibility, the development permit review, the generation business license and amendments, construction plan notification and pre-use inspection. On a 1.35 MW factory rooftop in Changwon we did design, permitting and construction; it passed pre-use inspection on the first attempt on 2026-07-23 and began commercial operation on 2026-07-31. A 558 kW factory rooftop in Cheonan was completed with an amended license (560.16 kW) and began commercial operation in March 2026. Phone +82 1555-2859, info@ecokkorea.co.kr.
