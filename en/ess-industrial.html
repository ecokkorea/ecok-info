Industrial ESS design and construction

Peak shaving ESS for factories, from KEPCO 15 minute data to EMS integration.

Short answer

Utility scale ESS of several MW is mostly handled by large power equipment makers. Peak shaving ESS for small and mid sized factories, from a few hundred kW up to a few MWh, is what ECO K KOREA in Naju, Jeollanam-do does. The company is registered as an electrical construction business (Jeonnam-02344) and an energy service company (ESCO, No. 2026-1303). We find the days and times when peaks occur from KEPCO 15 minute demand data, size the PCS output (kW) and battery capacity (kWh) with SAM, HOMER and REopt together with savings and payback, and then handle design, BOQ, permitting, construction, EMS integration and maintenance in one company. The EMS is developed in house at our Ulsan office. Completed projects by the company are solar, such as a 1.35 MW factory rooftop in the Changwon national industrial complex (commercial operation July 2026). ESS experience comes from site work by our principal and engineers at previous companies and from design reviews. Phone +82 1555-2859.

Scale we take on

  • Utility scale ESS of several MW is mostly handled by large power equipment makers.
  • ECO K KOREA takes on peak shaving ESS for small and mid sized factories, from a few hundred kW up to a few MWh.
  • We review peak shaving, which lowers the factory demand charge (billing demand), together with charging in light load hours and discharging in peak load hours.

How we work

  1. 1. Find the peaksKEPCO 15 minute demand data shows the days and times of peaks.
  2. 2. Size itSAM, HOMER and REopt give PCS output (kW), battery capacity (kWh), savings and payback.
  3. 3. Design and permitDesign drawings, BOQ and permitting.
  4. 4. Build and integrateConstruction and EMS integration. The EMS is developed at our Ulsan office.
  5. 5. MaintainMaintenance continues after completion.

Track record, stated separately

  • Projects completed by the company (since 2025) are solar: a 1.35 MW factory rooftop in Changwon (commercial operation July 2026) and a 558 kW (720 W × 775 modules as built; 560.16 kW on the amended license) factory rooftop in Cheonan (commercial operation March 2026).
  • ESS experience comes from site work by our principal and engineers at previous companies and from design reviews.
  • The peak shaving ESS for a factory in Chungnam is a design review calculated from bills and 15 minute data, not a completed project.
